# Prices and changing expectations

A stock price reflects what investors collectively require to own uncertain future cash flows. Prices can move when expected earnings change, when the required return changes, or when uncertainty changes.

## Surprises matter

A company can report growth and still disappoint if investors expected faster growth. Strong economic data can pressure valuations if it causes investors to expect higher interest rates.

## Common misconception

Good news is not automatically good for the stock price. The result must be compared with prior expectations and the price already paid for them.
