# Diversification and ETF overlap

Diversification spreads exposure across investments whose outcomes are not identical. The number of ticker symbols alone does not measure it.

## Look through the labels

Two funds can have different names and still own many of the same companies. Several individual stocks can also depend on the same sector, rate environment, or economic driver.

Correlation estimates historical co-movement over a stated sample. They can change across time and market conditions.

## Common misconception

Owning more positions does not always reduce risk. What matters is the size and relationship of the underlying exposures.
