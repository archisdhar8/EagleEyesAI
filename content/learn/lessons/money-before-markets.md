# Money before markets

An investment account is not a substitute for near-term financial stability. Before adding market risk, identify cash needs, expensive debt, and protections against fraud.

## A practical order of operations

- Protect essential near-term expenses with accessible cash.
- Capture an employer retirement match when available and suitable.
- Compare the known cost of high-interest debt with uncertain investment returns.
- Invest only money whose time horizon can tolerate fluctuations.

## Fraud and account safety

Promises of guaranteed returns, pressure to act immediately, requests to send assets to an unfamiliar person, and unverifiable credentials are warning signs. Use regulated providers, strong authentication, and independently verify claims.

## Common misconception

Waiting until every financial detail is perfect can delay useful action, but skipping liquidity and expensive debt can turn an ordinary market decline into a forced sale.
