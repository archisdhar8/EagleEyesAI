# Accounts, stocks, bonds, and funds

An account is a container. An investment is an asset held inside that container.

## Common containers

Taxable brokerage accounts and retirement accounts can hold many of the same assets, but taxes, contribution rules, and withdrawal restrictions differ. Those rules can change, so current IRS and plan documents matter.

## Common assets

- A stock represents an ownership interest in a company.
- A bond represents a lending agreement with an issuer.
- A fund pools many investments under one strategy.
- An ETF is a fund whose shares trade on an exchange.

## Common misconception

An ETF is not automatically diversified, inexpensive, or appropriate. Its holdings, strategy, concentration, liquidity, and expense ratio must be inspected.
