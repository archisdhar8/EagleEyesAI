# Macro, news, and evidence quality

Interest rates, inflation, growth, employment, credit, currencies, and commodities can affect companies through different channels. Their relevance depends on the company and portfolio exposure.

## Evidence hierarchy

Prefer dated primary data and company filings. Distinguish a released value from a forecast, a change from a surprise, and a historical relationship from a causal claim.

News sentiment summarizes language; it does not establish that an event changes long-term value. Prediction markets summarize contract prices under their own rules and liquidity conditions; they are one source of evidence rather than a complete forecast.

## Common misconception

Correlation means two variables moved together in the measured sample. It does not prove that one caused the other or that the relationship will persist.
