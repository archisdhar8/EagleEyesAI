# Risk and time horizon

Risk is not one number. EagleEyes separates willingness, capacity, and required risk because they can conflict.

## Three different questions

- Risk tolerance: how much loss and volatility you are emotionally willing to accept.
- Loss capacity: how much financial loss you can absorb without disrupting important needs.
- Required risk: how much investment growth a goal appears to require under its assumptions.

Time can improve the opportunity to recover from a decline, but it does not eliminate loss.

## Common misconception

Being young does not automatically make an aggressive portfolio suitable. A near-term home purchase, unstable income, expensive debt, or low tolerance for loss can reduce practical risk capacity.
