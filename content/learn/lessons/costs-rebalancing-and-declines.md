# Costs, rebalancing, and market declines

Returns that remain after fees, taxes, and implementation costs are more useful than returns shown before them.

## Fee drag

An expense ratio is charged by a fund each year as a percentage of assets. Small annual differences can compound over long periods.

## Rebalancing

Rebalancing returns an allocation toward intended ranges. It can be done with new contributions, gradually, or through immediate trades. Each path has different taxes, turnover, and timing risk.

## Market declines

A 30% decline requires more than a 30% gain to recover because the gain starts from a smaller base. A decline plan created before volatility can reduce reactive decisions.

## Common misconception

Rebalancing is not a claim that recent winners will fall. It is a risk-control decision tied to an allocation policy.
