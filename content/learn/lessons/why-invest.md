# Why save and invest?

Saving and investing solve different problems. Savings are designed for stability and access. Investments accept uncertainty in pursuit of long-term growth.

## Start with the time horizon

Money needed soon—such as an emergency reserve, rent, tuition, or a near-term purchase—should not depend on the stock market being up on a particular day. Money intended for a distant goal may have time to recover from market declines, but recovery is never guaranteed.

## Compounding and inflation

Compounding means earlier gains can themselves produce later gains. Inflation works in the opposite direction by reducing what a fixed amount of money can buy. Both effects build over time, and neither tells you which investment to choose.

## Common misconception

Investing is not a guaranteed upgrade from saving. The right tool depends on when the money is needed, how much loss can be absorbed, costs, taxes, and the range of possible outcomes.
